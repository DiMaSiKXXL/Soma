Frequently Asked Questions

The 30-Day Reset

  • What types of exercises does it include?
    This challenge includes somatic exercises, nervous system techniques, EFT tapping, self-massage, somatic sculpt, breathwork, and guided activities to help you release stored tension and regulate your system.
  • Do I need equipment?
    No equipment required! Just yourself and a quiet space. A pillow or blanket can add comfort, but everything works perfectly without any special gear.
  • What if I miss a day (or a few days)?
    Life happens! You can jump back in anytime without penalty. The program adapts to your schedule, and all past sessions remain available for you to complete at your convenience.
  • What if I feel emotional during or after a class?
    It's completely normal. Emotional releases are a natural part of the process. Allow yourself to feel without judgment—it's a sign that the practice is working on a deeper level.
  • What if I don’t feel anything?
    That's okay too! Everyone's journey is different. Some people feel immediate shifts, while others notice subtle changes over time. Trust the process and stay consistent.
  • Can I keep working out while doing this Challenge?
    Yes, you can maintain your regular workout routine. However, listen to your body—this challenge is designed to complement your fitness, not compete with it. You might even find you need more rest than usual.
  • What if I experience physical sensations like shaking, yawning, or soreness?
    These are common signs of energy release and nervous system regulation. Yawning, shaking, or tingling means your body is letting go of stored tension—embrace it as a positive sign.
  • What if I experience pain or discomfort?
    Pain is different from discomfort. If you feel sharp or persistent pain, stop immediately and consult a healthcare professional. Discomfort (like stretching or mild soreness) is normal—breathe through it.
  • Why are some days shorter than others?
    The program is intentionally designed with varying lengths. Shorter days offer active recovery and integration, while longer days dive deeper. This variation prevents burnout and optimizes results.
  • What time of day is best to do my class?
    Whenever you can be consistent! Morning sessions energize your day, evening practices help you unwind. Experiment to find what feels best for your body and schedule.
  • What if I’m pregnant or have limited mobility?
    Please consult your doctor before starting. Many exercises can be modified, but safety comes first. Reach out to our support team for personalized modifications and guidance.
  • What if I feel tired or drained after class?
    This is a sign your nervous system is recalibrating. Make sure to hydrate, rest, and eat nourishing foods. Fatigue usually subsides after the first week as your body adapts.
  • How long do I have access to the challenge?
    You get lifetime access to all challenge materials. Replay classes as many times as you want, whenever you want—no expiration dates or hidden fees.
  • Can I repeat the challenge after I finish?
    Absolutely! Most participants do. Each round reveals new layers of growth, and you'll likely experience the practices differently with fresh perspective and deeper embodiment.
  • Will I receive personal feedback on my progress?
    Yes! Our community platform allows you to share reflections and receive guidance. Premium members also get access to weekly live Q&A sessions and personalized check-ins.
